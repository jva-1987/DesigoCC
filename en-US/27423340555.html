Device Overview

In flex client, navigate to Powermanager root node > [Area] > [Device], to view the data generated by a device.

 

1

Limit violation timeline

Limit violation timeline shows the graphical representation of all the violations occurred in selected timeline.

For Limit violation timeline graph, X-axis shows the timeline selected. Y-axis of graph shows different types of violations with their severity.

To know the details of any specific violation, hover your mouse on violation. To view or hide data of violation in graph, click on the name of violation on top of the graph.

-  Zoom : Click to Zoom in and out of any particular area of the waveform, and click Exit zoom to close the zooming option.
-  Reset zoom : Resets the zoom functionality and returns it to its default state.
-  : Maximize and minimize the waveform, phasor, and harmonics window.

2

Cards
