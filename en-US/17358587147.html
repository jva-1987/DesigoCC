Replacement Day Editor

The Replacement Day Editor displays in the Apogee EQS tab when you select a replacement day or day shift in System Browser. From the Replacement Day Editor, you can:

  • add or remove a calendar date for a replacement day.
  • view a list of affected zones and events for a replacement day or day shift.
  • add or remove a day shift.
  • provide a description for a replacement day.
  • view replacement day events and zones in the Timeline.

Replacement Day

Displays the day shift and list of seven replacement day options. When you select an item, any associated zones and events display in the Affected Zones and Events section.

Affected Zones and Events

Displays a list of any zones and events associated with the item selected in the Replacement Day section.

  • When you click a zone or event, the schedule for that particular item displays in the Timeline.
  • When you double-click a zone or event, the Zone Editor or Event Editor displays the details for the selection.

Description

By default, Day Shift displays as the day shift descriptor, and the seven replacement days have a default description of Replacement Day [Number]. If desired, you can change the default description to a name that represents the replacement schedule or a situation (for example, "Christmas", "Alarm Checks", etc.) by typing a new name in the Descriptor field and then clicking the button. You can use up to 30 characters, upper- and lower-case letters, numbers, and spaces.

Calendar Entries

The fields in this section vary depending on if you select the day shift or a replacement day in the Replacement Day section.

Day Shift

When you have the day shift selected, day shift substitution(s) for a selected day's schedule display.

Item

Description

Dates

List of dates with the schedules you want to shift to another day. If desired, you can change a date by double-clicking its entry and clicking the drop-down arrow.

Shift To

Each date listed has an associated Shift To day. If desired, you can change the day by clicking the drop-down arrow.

X

Displays next to a schedule entry and when selected, deletes the associated entry from the day shift.

New

Adds the current day to the list of Dates.

Replacement Day

When you have a replacement day selected, a list of dates associated with the replacement day displays. If desired, you can change a date by clicking its entry, and then clicking the drop-down arrow.

Item

Description

New

Adds the current day to the selected replacement day schedule.

X

Displays next to a calendar entry and when selected, deletes the associated calendar entry from the replacement day.

Timeline (Replacement Days Only)

Displays the schedules for the selected zone or event in the Replacement Day section. Only schedules that are active within the 24-hour range for the current day display in the Timeline.
