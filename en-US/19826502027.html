Configuring a Fire WAN

Scenario

You want to configure Desigo CC for connecting to multiple XNETs via a Fire WAN network.
For background information, seeFire WAN Configuration Reference.

For system limits relating to the network configuration, search for XNET in Compliance Notice for UL Norms or Compliance Notice for ULC Norms.

Prerequisites

  • The Fire WAN extension module is installed and included in the active project.
  • The extension modules corresponding to the specific fire panels in use are installed and included in the active project. They can be either of the following:
    • FireFinder XLS/MXL
    • Desigo Fire Safety Modular UL
    • Cerberus Pro Modular UL
  • A communication card (NCC-2F or SNC USB) is installed on the Desigo CC server or FEP station to support the communication to the Fire WAN network. For information about the communication cards, see:
    • NCC-2F XNET Card Reference
    • SNC XNET Card Reference
