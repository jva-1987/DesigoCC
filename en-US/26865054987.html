Viewing Log Entries in Mobile Flex

  1. In System Browser, tap Application View > Log Viewer.
  2. The Log Viewer fetches all data and displays the log entries in Master pane.
  3. Select an entry from the Master pane.
  4. The details of the selected log entry displays in the Detailed pane.
  5. Tap  Back to close the details of log entry.
