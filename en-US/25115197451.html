Download Advanced Reports

  1. The History area displays the executed advanced reports in the selected format (PDF, Excel, or Both), and its preview displays in the preview area.
  • You can download advanced reports locally in the following ways:
    • In the History area, select the advanced report, adjacent to the advanced report, select  and then select Download.
      In the case of Both, if you perform the download operation on the history group, then all the child items are downloaded at once.
    • From the History area, select the advanced report that you want to download, and then, in the preview area, select Download  or select Download from the incorporated controls.
  1. The selected advanced report is downloaded locally.
