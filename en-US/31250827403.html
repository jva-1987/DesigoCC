XNET Fire Control Panels

This section provides background information on the integration of XNET fire control panels (FireFinder XLS/MXL, Desigo Fire Safety Modular, and Cerberus Pro Modular).

For configuration instructions, see the engineering step-by-step section and the network wizard. For a Fire WAN architecture, you can search for the required XNET configuration instructions in Configuring a Fire WAN.

For operator instructions, see the operating step-by-step section.

For UL/ULC compliance information, search for Compliance Notice for UL Norms or Compliance Notice for ULC Norms.
