System Overview

In the Flex Client, navigate to Powermanager, to view the overview of Power quality or violation status of devices at system level.

 

1

Specify the period for system overview to display the values accordingly. For more information, see Default period.
This period selection is also available in area and device overview.

2

Device status

Device status shows the number of devices in Limit violation, Warning, All OK.

Click More to see the details of devices in Limit violation, and Warning, All OK, No Data, and Under Evaluation.

Limit Violation: At least one device in the selected system is under violation.

Warning: At least one device in the selected system is under warning.

All OK: All the devices in selected system do not have any limit violation or warning.

No Data: Device does not have any PowerQuality data in selected timeline for selected system.

Under Evaluation: Powermanager is evaluating the data. This might take some time depending on the size of data.

3

Limit violations

X-axis shows number of devices violated and Y-axis shows areas available in selected systems.

Hover mouse on graph to know number of devices in limit violation and warning state under the area.
