Energy Management Tab Workspace

The Energy management tab contains default widgets for the configured device in the Installed Client. Based on the device type, it displays the device status, average values, total energy consumption, gauges for minimum and maximum values, energy consumption comparisons, and trends. In other words, it replicates the Operating view of the Installed Client.
The Energy management tab can be viewed from Application View > [System/Area/Sector/Device], for all supported devices. The devices includes standard devices, logical devices and imported devices such as Modbus, BACnet, Virtual, and IEC Generic devices. For these imported devices, the Energy Management tab with default widgets is displayed based on the Device Type Favorites configuration in the Installed Client.
You can create and edit widgets from this tab. For more information on creating and editing widgets, see Create a Widget under Create a Dashboard and Widget and Edit a Widget under Edit a Dashboard and Widget.
