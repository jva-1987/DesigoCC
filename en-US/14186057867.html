Migrating and Upgrading FC726 Cerberus Pro Fire Panels

Upgrading Control Panels from V6.0 to V7.0

  • Re-import of the V7.0 SiB-X metafile is required in Cerberus DMS.

Upgrade Cerberus DMS from Previous Versions

When you upgrade Cerberus DMS from V4.x/V5.0/V6.0 to V7.0, just follow the standard Cerberus DMS upgrade procedure.

Then, in the Cerberus DMS project, re-import the SiB-X metafile on each fire system to apply the latest library modifications.

Replacing NK823x as a router for the SafeDlink communication

NK823x used to be applied as a router for the fire networks in DMS8000 installations. In connection to Cerberus DMS, NK823x routing services can no longer support fire networks.
In case of migration, you can replace NK823x with Scalance S615. For more information, see the application guide Scalance S615 Router for SAFEDLINK Fire Networks (A6V13357580).
