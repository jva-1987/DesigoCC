FS92 Linked Station Ownership Concept

This ownership mode applies to:

  • Fire+Voice Panels: UL-864 10th ed. and ULC-S527

The norm requires a single point of control for voice systems. This single point of control must include the voice and fire operating interfaces, and optionally a management station.

For each fire network, you may associate the management station that is located in the point of control of the network and takes the ownership of the fire panels.
The association is made with the panels in the points of control.

The owner station has full control over sending control commands such as acknowledge and reset to the panels it owns, and it is the only station enabled to issue commands to those panels. Operators of other stations can see what is happening in the panels, but if they try to command them, they will receive a Command denied error.

If you are operating a station that is the owner of a panel, what you can see and control is entirely determined by your user privileges. All other stations at your site are not permitted to send any commands to that panel.
