Fire WAN Limits and Compatibility

For UL/ULC compliance information, see the Fire WAN Settings section in the Compliance Notice for UL Norms page or Compliance Notice for ULC Norms page.

Desigo Fire Safety Modular Compatibility

  • Zeus V14.xx or higher is required for Local FSI configuration (V14.05.xx or higher is recommended).
  • Zeus V15.00 (MP2.0) or higher is required for Global FSI configurations.

Cerberus Pro Modular Compatibility

  • Zeus V14.xx or higher is required for Local FSI configuration (V14.05.xx or higher is recommended).
  • Zeus V15.00 (MP2.0) or higher is required for Global FSI configurations.

XLS FireFinder Compatibility

  • Zeus V12.03 or higher is required for Local FSI configurations (only 4 queues are supported).
  • XLS FireFinder is not supported in Global FSI subnets.

NOTE: In the FS20M Zeus configuration (up to V15.00.xxxx), if the NFPA System Type is configured as Proprietary, the Self Restoring Troubles and Self Restoring Supervision options in the Behavior must not be selected.
