Reduce or Expand Summary Bar Height

  1. The System workspace displays on the screen along with the Layout settings.
  • By default, under Status bar height, the height of the Summary bar is reduced: .
    • To expand it, select .
  1. Summary bar height expands.
  • To reduce it again, select .

NOTE: The Summary bar reduces automatically below 1200 pixels, when the right panel is collapsed, or below 1600 pixels when the right panel is expanded.
If the Summary bar is already reduced because of low screen resolution, the icons to reduce or expand Summary bar will not be available.

Your user preferences are automatically saved when you exit the Flex Client application. They persist across sessions, but they are lost in case of project upgrade.
