Bypassing XNET System Devices

Scenario

You want to manually disable and then re-enable fire system devices to temporarily exclude them from the system.

  1. In System Browser, select Management View.
  2. Locate and expand the network in the Field Networks structure.
  3. In the control panel structure, click the module or the device you want to bypass (or re-enable).
  4. In the Extended Operation tab, click Disable or Enable.
    NOTE: Not all modules can be disabled.
  1. In a few seconds, the command executes and the new disabled/enabled status displays.
    NOTE: The exact terminology may vary where devices may display as Excluded/Included.
  2. A Trouble event will be displayed in Event List to remind all system operators that a device is bypassed.
