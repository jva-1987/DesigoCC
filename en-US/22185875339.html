Compliance Notice for ULC Norms

Some countries have specific requirements for how the user interface of a fire protection system appears and functions.
In Canada, the relevant norms are the Underwriters Laboratories of Canada (ULC) in the ULC S527 and ULC S576 specifications.

NOTICE TO USERS, INSTALLERS, AUTHORITIES HAVING JURISDICTION, AND OTHER INVOLVED PARTIES
This product incorporates field-programmable software. In order for the product to comply with the requirements in the Standard for Fire Alarm Systems, ULC S527, and in the Standard for Mass Notification Systems, ULC S576, certain programming features or options must be limited to specific values or not used at all as indicated in the following tables.

Journaling Printout Settings

ULC S527-compliant fire systems must provide a journaling line printer that meets the related performance requirements of printing 50 event-related registrations in 90 seconds.

You must modify your printer’s default settings and select a pre-defined ULC journaling template to ensure the printer meets the ULC S527 requirements.

The printer settings shown in the following specification sections refer to the line printer model EPSON LQ-2090 or LQ-2090II.

ULC Journaling Template Specifications

The ULC Templates are located at:
[installation drive:]\[installation folder]\[project]\profiles\JournalingUserTemplates.

You should use one of the ULC templates along with the line printer settings to ensure the required performance (50 Alarm-related events printed in 90 seconds).
The ULC Templates have a condensed layout which results in a faster printout.
They provide no page headers and footer, and include the following four data columns:

  • Cause
  • Device Text
  • Transition Time
  • Event Source

System Types and Configuration Options

System Type

Permitted in ULC S527 and ULC S576 ? (Y/N)

Settings Permitted in ULC S527 and ULC S576

NFPA 72
Local - Campus

Yes

The Desigo CC management station is in monitoring mode only. The remote fire panel nodes are configured as stand-alone 72 Local - Campus panels. Local control is through local keyboard and display modules (MKB/PMI).
The Desigo CC management station displays the current values of data points; however, it cannot directly acknowledge, silence, or reset a remote node.

- Control commands disabled in the Security section for the fire panels (define a scope for the entire XNET and set the Read-only property for User access to it).

NFPA 72
Local - Highrise

Yes

The Desigo CC management station is the primary control point for the XNET (a fire panel node or a collection of fire panel nodes). It is capable of block acknowledge, query, and control of the system. The remote fire panel nodes are configured as 72 Local - Highrise units and may contain their own annunciators (MKB/PMI). No local control is possible at a remote keyboard (MKB/PMI) unless its node is out of communication with the Desigo CC management station.

- Control commands enabled (default) in the Security section for the fire panels.

- XNET Block commands (Silence/Unsilence, Ack, Reset) enabled. See Configuring the XNET Network.

NFPA 72
