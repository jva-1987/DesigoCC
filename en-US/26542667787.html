Tasks Notes and Validation

If the Note dialog box displays, operators are prompted to enter a note and explain why starting, reverting, closing, or aborting a task, as well as, when modifying the task due date. This note will also be logged for further analysis. To view the log, go to the System tab > System Browser > Log Viewer.

The operator’s notes will be displayed in the Notes expander.

This expander contains a maximum of ten notes, and you can enter up to 500 characters..

Validation of the Operations on the Operator Tasks

Operators might be prompted to validate their operations on the tasks. In particular, the validation comment is taken as an operator task note when editing, starting, closing, aborting, or deleting tasks, as well as, when doing revert actions. If a validation comment is required, operators are not prompted to enter the typical task note.
