ULC-compliant Access Levels

According to ULC norms, all users must be assigned to specific access levels. ULC-compliant access levels can be managed by Desigo CC predefined user groups.

ULC-compliant Access Levels

Access Level

Intended Persons

Desigo CCUser Group

1

Those having a general responsibility for safety supervision, who might be expected to investigate and initially respond to a fire alarm or trouble signal.

Fire Operators

2

Those who have a specific responsibility for safety, and who are trained to operate the control unit.

N/A

3

Those who are trained and certified by the manufacturer and authorized by the authority having jurisdiction to both repair and maintain the control unit, or to alter its specific data or firmware, thereby changing its basic mode of operation.

Project Administrators
