APOGEE Point Editor Workspace

The APOGEE BACnet Point Editor displays in System Browser, when you are in Management View > Field Networks > [Network name] > [Field Panel name] > Points folder.

You must be in Engineering mode.

  • When you click on the Point folder – you are in Create Mode.
  • When you are in the Point folder and click on a point – you are in Read Mode.
