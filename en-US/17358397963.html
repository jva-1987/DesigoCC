Timeline Viewer

The Timeline Viewer allows you to view the details of multiple management station and field panel schedules simultaneously, spanning a range of time. To do this, System Manager must be in Operating Mode.

In the Timeline Viewer you can:

  • add schedules from the Apogee EQS tab either automatically or manually
  • select specific preset time periods to display
  • view details of an event schedule in the Timeline Viewer
  • use color indicators and intervals (Default, Normal, Exception, and Inactive) to visually indicate whether or not something has been scheduled for that period
  • control the displayed time span of schedules using the Time Range scrollbar

For comprehensive information about using the Timeline Viewer, see Overview of Timeline Viewer.
