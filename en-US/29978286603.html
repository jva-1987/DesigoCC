Archive Groups

  • In addition to the existing Desigo CC archive groups, the archive group GPM_ Long_Term_Miscellaneous, allows you to archive instantaneous measurement points into the long-term storage using the Archive Group and the Filter Group field values.
  • The new miscellaneous archive group is available in the project if Desigo CC is upgraded to or installed with the latest version.
    By default, in the Extended Operations tab, Storage 2 is mapped to the miscellaneous archive group. It is recommended to create a new Storage and link it to the miscellaneous archive group. This feature is applicable to physical (supported in Modbus, BACnet, IEC61850, and OPC protocols), virtual and logical devices.
  • NOTE: The GPM_Long_Term_Miscellaneous archive group must be linked to any of the available storage to avoid data loss.
  • The Archive Group field allows you to select archive group for the single or multiple measurement points from the Measurement Points expander when creating a device instance or after device creation. This functionality is supported for physical, virtual, and logical devices except for the Manual Measuring Device.

For related instructions ,see Selecting the Archive Group for Devices.
