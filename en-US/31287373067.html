Edit the Energy Management Tab

You can edit the Energy management tab to edit widgets, add widgets, and restore the settings.

  • In the Energy management tab, click .
  • The Energy management tab switches to the edit mode.

Do any of the following:

  • Click Create widget to add a new widget.
    When creating widgets, the device that supports energy management is considered and the data points of that device display.
  • Edit the existing widgets (as required).
  • Click Restore default settings to retain the default configuration of the Energy management tab.
    Save the Energy management tab after editing.
