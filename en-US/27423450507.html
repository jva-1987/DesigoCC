Area Overview

In flex client, navigate to Powermanager root node > [Area], to view the overview of PowerQuality or violation status of devices at an area level.

 

1

Device status

Device status shows the number of devices in Limit Violation, Warning, and All OK.

Click More to see the details of devices in Limit Violation, and Warning, All OK, No Data, and Under Evaluation.

Limit Violation: At least one device in the selected area is under violation.

Warning: At least one device in the selected area is under warning.

All OK: All the devices in the selected area do not have any violation or warning.

No Data: Device does not have any PowerQuality data in selected timeline for selected area.

Under Evaluation: Powermanager is evaluating the data. This might take some time depending on size of data.

2

Limit violations

Select the violation from dropdown menu to get number of devices device in Limit violation and warning.

X-axis shows percentage(%) of violation for all devices and Y-axis shows number of devices in Limit violation and warning for selected timeline.

The device list shows list of all devices in violation and warning according to range of percentage(%).

3

Voltage events

Number of Voltage events shows the number Voltage events, Sag, Swell, and Interruption for selected timeline.

Graph shows bubble representation of events occurred over a period of time.

The size of bubble depends on the selected timeline, and number of events occurred in selected timeline. More events will have bigger size of balloon.
