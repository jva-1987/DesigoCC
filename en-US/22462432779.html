Night

The Night expander enables you to configure heating and cooling modes and allowed temperature differential for night operation.

Item

Description

Night Heating Mode

Defines the night heating mode that is used during vacancy periods to keep the temperature above the vacancy setpoint.

> Click the drop-down arrow and select a mode.

Night Cooling Mode

Defines the night cooling mode that is used during vacancy periods to keep the temperature above the vacancy setpoint.

> Click the drop-down arrow and select a mode.

Differential

Defines the allowed degree deviation from the setpoint during vacancy. The differential is used for both heating and cooling to keep the temperature close to the vacancy setpoint during the vacancy periods.

> Type a value for the differential; decimals are allowed.
