Copy a Zone, Event, or Replacement Day Schedule

Once you create zone, event, or replacement day schedules, you can copy them to reduce unnecessary repetition when you enter data. For example, copying is useful when a building has the same control requirements among floors.

NOTE:
When you copy a zone schedule over to another zone, any overrides the zone may have are not copied.

  1. System Manager is in Engineering mode.
  2. You have the zone or event to which you want to copy an item displaying in the Apogee EQS tab.
  1. In System Browser, select Application View.
  2. From the Zone Editor or Event Editor in the Apogee EQS tab, open the Schedules expander.
  3. Do one of the following to copy a schedule to the active item:
    • To copy a zone schedule: Navigate to Applications > Schedules > APOGEE Zones and drag-and-drop the zone over the Copy To field in the Schedules expander.
    • To copy an event schedule: Navigate to Applications > Schedules > APOGEE Events and drag-and-drop the event over the Copy To field in the Schedules expander.
    • To copy a replacement day schedule: Navigate to Applications > Schedules > APOGEE Replacement Days and drag-and-drop the replacement day over the Copy To field in the Schedules expander.
      NOTE: Day shift schedules cannot be copied.
  4. Click Copy.
  5. The schedule displays in the Timeline for the current item.
  6. Click Saved .
  1. The copied schedule is saved.
