Handle Display Issues

Misaligned Summary Bar

If Summary bar appears misaligned, do the following:

  1. Open Windows Display settings.
  2. Locate Scale and Layout.
  3. Under Change the size of texts, apps, and other items, select 125% (Recommended).
  4. Restart the Flex Client station to apply changes.
