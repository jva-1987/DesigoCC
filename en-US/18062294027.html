ULC S527 Management Stations Requirements

ULC S527-listed Computer Specifications

The following UL 864/ULC S527-listed computer models can be used for Desigo CC fire installations:

  • Comark Dual Xeon Server (Siemens Model UHW-CATD-01): a dual-CPU, server-class computer
  • Comark Xeon Server (Siemens Model UHW-CATC-01): a server-class computer
  • Comark I7 Desktop (Siemens Model UHW-CATB-01/UHW-CATB-02): a server-class computer
  • Comark EnduraNodeTMFX (Siemens Model UHW-CATA-01): a compact computer

NOTE 1: See also BSIU HW Req and SW Spec.
NOTE 2: Interconnecting wiring between a stationary computer and the computer's keyboard, video monitor, touch screen, or mouse-type device should not exceed 2.5 m.
NOTE 3: 43" monitors (UHW-DISP-LM-TS/UHW-DISP-LM-NT) are supplied with wall mounting bracket kit # 50-11452-003 (included in monitor box). The Comark user guide, part # 05-14263-300-A (included in the monitor box), describes how to install the monitor using this wall mount kit.

Uninterruptible Power Supply

If an Uninterrupted Power Supply (UPS) is required for your system configuration then the UPS must meet the requirements of CSA-C22.2 No. 107.3, Standard for Uninterruptable Power Systems and the applicable requirements of ULC-S527.

NOTES:

  • The UPS shall be installed in accordance with CSA C22.1, Canadian Electrical Code, Part I, Safety Standard for Electrical Installations, Section 46 – Emergency Power Supply, Unit Equipment, Exit Signs, and Life Safety Systems.
  • For maintenance and repair, a means shall be provided to disconnect the UPS while maintaining continuity of power to the fire signal receiving and transmitting equipment, if applicable.
  • According to the ULC requirements, 4 hours of standby operation is required if there is a backup generator. If there is no backup generator, then 24 hours of standby operation is required.
  • The UPS system must provide a trouble signal to the fire signal receiving and transmitting equipment indicating the following abnormal conditions:
    • UPS switches from the primary power source to the secondary power source.
    • UPS trouble.
  • UPS must be installed in a secured location.
  • On employing the means for disconnecting the UPS while maintaining continuity of power to the fire signal receiving and transmitting equipment to perform maintenance and repair, shall result in a non-latching supervisory signal or trouble signal at the fire signal receiving and transmitting equipment.
  • The input and output of a UPS system must have a means of permanent connections. The permanent connections must conform to CSA-C22.1, Canadian Electrical Code, Part I, Safety Standard for Electrical Installations, Section 32.
