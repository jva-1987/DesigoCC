View Energy Management Details

  1. In the System Browser, select Application View.
  2. Go to Powermanager > [Area] > [Device].
    Energy management tab can also be viewed from Application View > [System/Area/Sector/Device].
  1. The Energy management tab displays widgets for the configured device in the Installed Client. Based on the device type, it displays the device status, average values, total energy consumption, gauges for minimum and maximum values, energy consumption comparisons, and trends.

The Energy management tab is not available for POC1000 and its sub-devices. If you select the Energy management tab for this devices, an error message displays.
