Graphics Settings

By default, any graphic that is selected will also show graphical items of the "ancestor" elements above it in the hierarchy.

This behavior can be modified through the Exclude Ancestor Graphics option in Account Settings

Exclude Ancestor Graphics

Active

Only display graphics related to the current selected node

Inactive

Default. Display graphics of the selected item and all graphics of its ancestors in any of the views.

Changes to Exclude Ancestor Settings will only be visible when the session is restarted. This includes a change made in the Flex Client or other DCC sessions.
