Change Theme and Text Representation

From the Layout settings you can change Flex theme and how to label objects according to your preferences.

Choose Light or Dark Mode

  • Under Theme, select one of the following:
    • Light
    • Dark (high contrast theme)
    • Auto (automatic detection of the operating system mode; the web browser theme has no effect on Flex Client mode.)

Change Text Representation

  • Under Text representation, choose the desired label.
    NOTE: In Flex web/desktop app, text representation is not restored to default when resetting the System workspace layout.
