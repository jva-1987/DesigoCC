Check Licenses in Mobile Flex

This section provides instructions to check Desigo CC licenses in the mobile Flex web app.

 

  1. Tap  > System.
  2. List view displays on the screen.
  3. Tap .
  4. System Browser displays on the screen.
  5. Tap  next to Application View and then choose Management View.
  6. Tap  next to the Management System.
  7. Tap  next to the Servers.
  8. Tap  next to the Main Server.
  9. Tap License.
